Carnero Video — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Carnero Video ("the app") is a private desktop tool used by its owner to prepare and publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What data the app accesses from TikTok

When the owner connects a TikTok account and grants permission, the app uses these TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic — the account's display name, avatar and identifier, to show which account a post will go to.
  • video.publish — to publish a video to the owner's TikTok account, only after the owner reviews the post, chooses who can see it and presses "Post".
  • video.list — to read the public counts of the owner's own TikTok videos (views, likes, comments, shares), so the owner can see how the videos perform.

The app never posts without the owner's explicit action, never deletes or edits content on TikTok, and does not access other users' data.

How the data is stored

All data stays on the owner's own computer: the account's name and identifier, the posts the app made and their counts are stored in a local database. The sign-in tokens TikTok issues are kept in the app's protected local credential store and are never displayed or written to logs. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app's author.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share TikTok data with anyone. The only party that receives data from the app about TikTok is TikTok itself, when the owner publishes a video or reads the counts of the owner's own videos.

Retention and removal

The data is kept on the owner's computer for the owner's records. Access can be withdrawn at any time by disconnecting the app in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions), after which the app can no longer publish or read anything.

Children

The app is not intended for, and is not used by, anyone under 18.
